    $GREED — How to Play

    Goal

    Be the first player to reach 5000 points. Roll six dice, bank scoring combos, and push your luck — but if a roll scores nothing, you GREED OUT and lose everything banked that turn.

    Scoring & Combos

    1× G503× G500
    1× D1003× R400
    3× $6003× E300
    4× D1000$GREED / Full set1000
    6 of a kind5000extra matching die +50 / +100

    Dice: E light green, E blue, G yellow, R red, $ dark green, D black. Only G and D score as single dice. Every other symbol needs 3+ to score. A 4-of-a-kind pays the trio value plus any extra single (e.g. 4× G = 500 + 50 = 550).

    $GREED (Full set) needs both coloured E's. To earn the 1000-point full set you must have one of every symbol in a single roll — including both the light-green E and the blue E. Two E's of the same colour do not complete the set. Note: the two E colours are separate entities — a 3-of-a-kind must be 3× light-green E or 3× blue E, never a mix of colours.

    How a Turn Works

    1. Roll all six dice. Tap the scoring dice to set them aside.
    2. Bank your points, or roll the remaining dice for more.
    3. Set-aside dice are safe; only the dice you reroll are at risk.
    4. If a reroll shows zero scoring dice → GREED OUT: the whole turn is forfeited (0 scored, passed on).
    5. Roll all six again (a "hot roll") and the turn continues.

    Ending the Game (First to 5000)

    When any player crosses 5000, the final round begins. Every other player gets one last turn to try to overtake. Highest total after those final turns wins.

    Strategy — roll bust on purpose to delay the end: If you're near 5000 but an opponent is also close, banking a thin lead (e.g. you're on 4900, roll +200 → 5100) opens the final round and hands them a free last turn to beat you. Instead, reroll your remaining dice — if you bust, the turn ends at 0 and the final round is not triggered yet, buying you another go next round to land a bigger, safer lead.

    Single-Player vs AI

    Add your name as Player 1, then flip the Ai Opponent slider on 1–3 seats (named Claude, Gwen, Gemma). The AI rolls, banks the best scoring set, and uses positional strategy near 5000 — including the deliberate bust above.

    🧬 CHAOS — How to Play

    In CHAOS mode a 7th die — the Chaos Die — is rolled automatically right after you bank (never on a bust). It lands on one of the faces below. Faces marked “On your next roll…” arm a power you use on your following turn; faces marked “Immediate!” happen at once.

    The Chaos Faces

    Buttons that appear mid-game

    • 🕵️ MULLIGAN REROLL — only shows after you rolled an Insider Info and have banked/set dice. Click it to reroll your current throw for free. If the reroll scores nothing, you keep your first roll's score instead of busting.
    • 💼 UN-BANK & REROLL — only shows after you rolled a Hostile Takeover and have set-aside dice. Click it to send your last set-aside dice back into the tray and reroll them for a better result.
    • 🧬 (die badge) — when Mutation is armed, a non-scoring die shows a 🧬 badge. Tap it, pick a new face, and it changes to match a scoring die.
    • 🥶 (die badge) — when Frozen Asset is armed, any fresh die shows a 🥶 badge. Tap it to pre-lock that die as a $ (600) or G (50) before you'd normally need to roll it.
    • 🫳 (die badge) — a die stolen via Short Squeeze is marked 🫳; it's just a normal die on your side now.

    Quick reminders

    • Bank or reroll with the normal 💰 BANK / 🎲 ROLL REMAINING buttons — CHAOS adds powers on top, it doesn't replace them.
    • Your first bank each game must be ≥ 500 (bank-or-bust). Chaos powers don't change that.
    • Need the core rules (scoring, GREED OUT, final round)? Open 📖 How to Play / Rules from the setup screen.
